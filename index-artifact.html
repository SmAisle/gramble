How to Play

  • Daily game: everyone gets the same words each day, and your first game counts. After that you can replay today's list for free (it doesn't change your score), or play new lists with ⭐ Plus.
  • Quick 10: you start with 50 credits and a list of 10 words. Find them all before your money runs out. Every spin costs 5 credits.
  • Clear bonus: clear all 10 and you get +50 credits. Then choose: Cash out and bank your money, or Let it ride into the High Roller 10, 10 more words, played with the money you have. The machine helps less, so they're harder. Find all 10 for a +100 credit bonus. Go broke and you bank nothing today. You can cash out any time during the High Roller.
  • Lifetime winnings: what you bank each day adds up. It's just for bragging rights.
  • Spin: tap Spin (or pull the lever). Five reels land on letters.
  • Lock: tap a reel (or its tile) to lock its letter 🔒. Locked letters stay put when you spin again. Tap again to unlock it.
  • Double down: with four letters locked and one reel left, you can spin for 10 credits instead of 5. If the word lands on that spin, it pays double. It's riskier, though: on a double down spin the machine helps a little less.
  • Hints: words that still fit your locked letters light up, and the letters you already have turn gray. For your first 2 words each game, 💡 Hints make the letters worth keeping glow after each spin, and the message tells you what to do. You can turn Hints off in the menu, and they stay off.
  • Tiles: the letters you lock show as tiles under the machine. Drag a tile left or right to rearrange them and try spelling your words. Tap a tile to unlock it.
  • Find a word: when the five letters on the line can spell a word on your list, in any order, it's found! It's crossed off and pays 2 credits a point (Scrabble values). STONE pays 10 credits.
  • Jackpot: spell a list word on a spin with nothing locked and it pays double. It is rare, maybe once in 10 games.
  • Bonus words: if the letters spell a real word that isn't on your list, you can cash it in for half pay (once per game), or pass and keep your letters. It doesn't count toward your 20.
  • The machine helps: the reels only carry letters from words still on your list, and they lean toward words that fit what you're holding. Words that fit your held letters light up yellow.
  • Win by finding all 20. The game ends if you can't afford a spin. You can also cash out from the menu.
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in so we can count players: the day, how many games you played and the game version. Never your name, contacts or location. No real money is involved.
