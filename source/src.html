How to Play

  • Daily game: everyone gets the same 20 words each day, and your first game counts. After that you can replay today's list for free (it doesn't change your score), or play new lists with ⭐ Plus.
  • Goal: find all 20 words on your list before your money runs out. You start with $10, and every spin costs 50¢.
  • Spin: tap Spin (or pull the lever). Five reels land on letters.
  • Lock: tap a reel (or its tile) to lock its letter 🔒. Locked letters stay put when you spin again. Tap again to unlock it.
  • Hints: words that still fit your locked letters light up, and the letters you already have turn gray.
  • Tiles: the letters you lock show as tiles under the machine. Drag a tile left or right to rearrange them and try spelling your words. Tap a tile to unlock it.
  • Find a word: when the five letters on the line can spell a word on your list, in any order, it's found! It's crossed off and pays 20¢ a point (Scrabble values). STONE pays $1.00.
  • Jackpot: spell a list word on a spin with nothing locked and it pays double. It is rare, maybe once in 10 games.
  • Bonus words: if the letters spell a real word that isn't on your list, you can cash it in for half pay (once per game), or pass and keep your letters. It doesn't count toward your 20.
  • The machine helps: the reels only carry letters from words still on your list, and they lean toward words that fit what you're holding. Words that fit your held letters light up yellow.
  • Win by finding all 20. The game ends if you can't afford a spin. You can also cash out from the menu.
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in so we can count players: the day, how many games you played and the game version. Never your name, contacts or location. No real money is involved.
