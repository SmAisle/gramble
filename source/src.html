How to Play

  • Daily game: everyone gets the same words each day, and your first game counts. After that you can replay today's list for free (it doesn't change your score), or play new lists with ⭐ Plus.
  • Quick 10: you start with 50 coins and a list of 10 words. Find them all before your coins run out. Every deal or hit costs 5 coins.
  • Clear bonus: clear all 10 and you get +50 coins. Then choose: Bank it to keep your coins, or Keep going into the Bonus 10, 10 more words, played with the coins you have. The deck helps less, so they're harder. Find all 10 for a +100 coin bonus. Go broke and you bank nothing today. You can bank it any time during the Bonus 10.
  • Lifetime winnings: what you bank each day adds up. It's just for bragging rights.
  • Deal: tap Deal and you get five letter cards.
  • Hold and hit: tap a card to hold it. Then tap Hit: held cards stay, and the others are replaced with new cards. Tap a held card again to let it go.
  • Power play: with four cards held and one to go, a Power play? box appears above Hit. Tap Yes and your next hit costs 10 coins instead of 5. If the word lands on that hit, it pays double, and so does a bonus word. It's riskier, though: on a power play hit the deck helps a little less.
  • Hints: words that still fit your held cards light up, and the letters you already have turn gray. For your first 2 words each game, 💡 Hints make the cards worth holding glow after each deal, and the message tells you what to do. You can turn Hints off in the menu, and they stay off.
  • Find a word: when your five cards can spell a word on your list, in any order, it's found! It's crossed off and pays 2 coins a point (letter values shown on each card). STONE pays 10 coins.
  • Jackpot: get dealt a list word with nothing held and it pays double. It is rare, maybe once in 10 games.
  • Bonus words: if the letters spell a real word that isn't on your list, you can cash it in for half pay (once per game), or pass and keep your letters. It doesn't count toward your 20.
  • The deck helps: it only carries letters from words still on your list, and it leans toward words that fit what you're holding. In the Quick 10, every deal without a word makes it help a little more, so bad luck never lasts long. Words that fit your held letters light up yellow.
  • Win by finding all 20. The game ends if you can't afford a deal. You can also bank it from the menu.
  • The website version sends an anonymous check-in so we can count players: the day, how many games you played and the game version. Never your name, contacts or location. No real money is involved.
